About Bank2Tally

Bank2Tally was built to solve a problem every accountant knows well: manually re-typing bank statement entries into Tally is slow, repetitive, and error-prone. We built a converter that reads your bank statement — PDF, Excel or CSV — and turns it into clean, ledger-mapped Tally vouchers in minutes instead of hours.

Who it's for

  • Practicing Chartered Accountants handling multiple client books
  • In-house accountants reconciling company bank accounts
  • Small business owners who manage their own Tally entries

Who built it

Bank2Tally is developed and maintained by Verma Consultancy Services, focused on practical automation tools for Indian accounting workflows.

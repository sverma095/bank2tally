Bank Statement to Tally Converter

Bank2Tally is a bank statement to Tally converter that turns your PDF, Excel, or CSV bank statement into Tally-ready vouchers within minutes — no manual data entry required.

How the conversion works

  • Upload your bank statement (PDF, scanned PDF, Excel or CSV).
  • Bank2Tally auto-detects columns and maps transactions to ledgers.
  • Duplicate entries are flagged before import.
  • Export as Tally XML, or push directly into Tally Prime.

Supported banks

HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, PNB and other major Indian banks. See bank-specific guides: HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak.

Why accountants use Bank2Tally

Manual entry of bank statements into Tally is slow and error-prone. Bank2Tally automates ledger mapping and reconciliation, cutting the time to prepare a month's bank vouchers from hours to minutes, while reducing mismatches during bank reconciliation.

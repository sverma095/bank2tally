Frequently Asked Questions

Which file formats does Bank2Tally support?

PDF bank statements (including scanned PDFs via OCR), Excel (.xls/.xlsx), and CSV files.

Which banks are supported?

HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Central Bank, Federal Bank, IOB and other major Indian banks. See bank-specific guides.

Does Bank2Tally work with Tally Prime?

Yes — export Tally XML and import via Gateway of Tally, or push directly to Tally Prime through the Tally Gateway when the extension is installed. See our Tally Prime import guide.

Is Bank2Tally free?

Yes, currently free for a limited time with no sign-up required. See pricing.

Is my bank statement data safe?

Statements are processed per session for conversion only and are not shared with third parties.

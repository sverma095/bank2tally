Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we process

When you upload a bank statement (PDF, Excel or CSV), Bank2Tally reads it in order to generate Tally-ready vouchers and XML export files. Registered accounts store profile information (name, email, company) via our authentication provider.

Guest / free usage

If you use Bank2Tally without signing in, your import history is stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device and is not uploaded to our servers.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data to third parties. Statement content is used solely to perform the conversion you requested.
